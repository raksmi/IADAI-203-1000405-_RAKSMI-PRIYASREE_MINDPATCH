MindPatch
Find your balance. Grow at your own pace.

MindPatch is an AI-assisted digital wellness planner concept designed to help students build mindful technology habits.

CRS: Artificial Intelligence
Prototype
Open the MindPatch Figma prototype
Core Features
1. Mood Logging
2. Screen-Time Dashboard
3. AI-Generated Detox Planner
4. Gratitude Journal
5. Habit Tracker with Rewards
Research
A Google Forms survey was conducted. The supplied response export contains 14 responses. Findings included:
- 7 respondents reported 4–6 hours of daily screen time.
- 6 respondents often checked their phones while studying or working.
- The average perceived effect of screen habits on daily routine was 3.8/5.
These are self-reported results from a small sample and should not be generalised to all students.

AI Logic
The proposed planner uses mood, screen time, time of day and routine patterns to suggest optional breaks. Its logic is simulated for this project; it is not a deployed AI model.
Project Stages
- Stage 1 – Research: Secondary research, survey analysis, competitor review, empathy maps and personas.
- Stage 2 – Ideation: SCAMPER/mind mapping, six wireframes and user journey.
- Stage 3 – Prototype: High-fidelity Figma prototype completed.
- Stage 4 – AI Simulation: Decision flow, pseudocode and five-day adaptation example.
- Stage 5 – Testing: Usability testing, two design iterations and project documentation.
